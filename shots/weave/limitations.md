## Open after review (polish, not blocking)

- **The knob face is partly covered while she turns it** (64–76% of the inner face at grip and turn). The turn reads through the meter needle and the canopy, not through the knob's own pointer. A pinch from below, or a larger knob scale, would fix it.
- **The upper arm crosses the chin at the grip** (172 px of arm over the head part). It reads as reaching across, but the profile is briefly broken.
- **The torso is a front view and the head is a profile.** This is a collage mix from the generated sheet, and it is visible.
- **The AI plate is held still.** The source video moves for only about 2 s, so the set does not move apart from camera parallax and the canopy.
- **Only two crowd figures react.** The rest of the crowd is one merged mask in the plate's segmentation, and it is relit, not animated.
- **The archive is 320×240.** The console is upscaled 4× and its grain is kept rather than invented away. It looks like a cut-up photograph, which is the intent, but it is soft next to the cardboard.
- **Word times come from faster-whisper (small).** They are within about 0.1 s by ear but are not hand-checked against the waveform.
- **The canopy mapping is a single homography masked to the vault.** The real canopy curves, so the archive's perspective is approximately right, not exact.
