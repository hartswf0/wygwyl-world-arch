Every cut puppet part of every character, side by side at one true scale: each card is drawn at its real size in cm (its file px ÷ its sheet's px/cm, which was measured against the character's skeleton from their height). The faint lines are every 10 cm. Hover or tap a card for its pivot (green) and sockets (red); “missing” shows the kit cells a character still lacks. Painted = a painting of this character; composited = recoloured or pasted from other paintings by the v2 kit generator; coded = drawn by a program. Cards with no measured scale are drawn 40 cm tall with a dashed edge.
