Every poem staged in the Cut-Out Suite: 80 scenes, 21.8 minutes of the reading. Puppets are sized by each set's solved scale, beats land on the words, and archive film opens in surfaces of the set, labelled as long as it shows.

"Pass" means the render's measurements hold (foot slide, reach, contact, scale, bodies, EX facing, off-frame, ground, word sync), not that the scene is good. Known limits: 6 scenes stand in for plates we still need (01.4, 05.2, 05.5, 13.1, 13.5, 13.6); many archive picks are weak in meaning because only 2,292 of 15,149 clips are cached; scenes 03.1, 03.2, 03.3, 03.4 and 03.6 have no bodies to measure.
