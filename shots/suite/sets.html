Sets every scene as layers you can stage in · scale solved · archive grabbable

Each of the 80 scenes in the breakdown gets one set: a plate (figure-free where we have one; figures the plate already had are painted out and logged), cut by measured depth and ADE20K class into planes P6 sky · P5 far · P4 mid · P3 ground · [P2 puppets] · P1 near · P0 occluders, a solved scale (where a 170 cm puppet stands and how big it is at every depth), the surfaces where an archive film can open, and ranked archive candidates for each. All coordinates are stage pixels, 1920×1080. Data: shots/suite/data/sets/<scene>.json; audit: AUDIT_sets.md; archive search: shots/suite/lib/archive_grab.py.

Status: good = the plate is the set and has no figures in it (scale is graded on its own) · usable = stands in, with the reason given · needs a plate = nothing we have is the set. Scale confidence: high (≥2 known-size objects agree with the ground plane) · medium (plane fit, ≤1 reference, camera height plausible) · low (camera height assumed at 1.5 m, or the plane disagrees with the picture: flags given) · assumed (no plane: stage calibration).
