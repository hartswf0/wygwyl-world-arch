What this is

A staging tool for the cut-out film. A scene is code: frame = draw(scene.json, t). Open a scene and its set planes, the reading for that scene with its word times, and its cast load together. Block puts puppets on the ground plane; their size comes from where their feet are (horizon + the set's measured px/cm), never from a slider. Act places beats on words and gives puppets goals; the solver turns both into footsteps, IK reaches and held gestures. Check draws every frame and measures it. Export writes the scene pack.

Solver

Puppets are skeletons in world centimetres, projected through one level pinhole per set. Walks are footstep plans: plants fixed in the world, swing arcs, a pelvis lowered until both legs reach (leg IK). Reaches and gestures are two-bone IK with a pole; contact targets are points on set surfaces or sockets on other puppets. Puppets with only replacement cards hop-step: the card moves only while lifted. Goals (go to, keep distance, sway to the beat, wander, walk with, face) compile into dashed beats you can read.

What is measured, what is missing

The checks run on the drawn frame: sole points and palms come from the same matrices that draw the cards; scale and occlusion come from each puppet drawn alone into a mask. Sources in use are listed under Export; anything marked STAND-IN is waiting on lanes A–C.
