How these are made

Each clip is an Acting Arena act re-solved from the Bolas Crash Lab 3D joints onto one character built from the cut poem-kit parts: bone directions from the source, bone lengths from the character (cm), legs by IK to the foot card's ankle brad, cards chained brad-to-brad, feet pinned while the source foot is down. Checks are measured on the drawn cards. Source: shots/stage/anim/retarget.py, rigs: rigs.py, per-character notes: NOTES.md.
