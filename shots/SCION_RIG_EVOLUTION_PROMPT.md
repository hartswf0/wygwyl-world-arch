### 1. DEFINE WHAT MAKES AN ARTICULATED CHARACTER EXCEPTIONAL

```
◻ CHARACTER THESIS
→ [Identifies]      → ◻ SILHOUETTE, WEIGHT & GAZE IDENTITY
→ [Requires]        → ◻ ZERO-SEAM TOPOLOGY & VIEW COHERENCE
→ [Is Demonstrated] → ◻ ACROSS 360° YAW & FULL GAIT CYCLE
→ [Is Tested Across]→ ◻ ALL 10 CANONICAL RIGS & 54 RANGE TESTS
```

Establish a concise character and biomechanical thesis before batch assembly:

- **What the viewer must immediately read:** Who the character is (Poet, EX, Woman/Muse, Child Age 7, Mother, Elder, DJ), where they are looking (`front`, `threequarter`, `profile`, `back`), where their center of mass sits over their support base, and what internal muscular effort drives their gesture.
- **What makes handmade gouache cut-out rigging particular:** The tactile charm of painted paper, brass brads, and layered wardrobe combined with the living weight of real gravity, antagonistic tendons, joint ligaments, and cylindrical 2.5D turn parallax.
- **What ruins the illusion (and must be eliminated on sight):**
  1. Mislabeled slots from heuristic sheet cutting (e.g., internal 3-grommet triangular `hipcore` armatures or open hands assigned to `pelvis`, shins assigned to `thigh`, front vests duplicated into `profile` torso slots, or left-pointing shoes assigned to right-facing profile walks).
  2. Exposed mounting tongues at wrists, ankles, and necks where child extremities are drawn naively over parent cuffs.
  3. Rectangular alpha or skin-tone patches around facial features on blank heads.
  4. Sideways (`90°`-rotated) head crops or view mismatches (a profile head paired with front eyes, or a front torso paired with a sagittal walk).
  5. Floating, single-point "ice-skating" feet that lack heel-strike, plantar arch roll, and metatarsal toe-off.

### 2. ESTABLISH A BAR THAT SURVIVES PLAYBACK AND LOUPE INSPECTION

```
◻ QUALITY REFERENCE
→ [Demonstrates]    → ◻ SPECIFIC BIOMECHANICAL OR AESTHETIC ACHIEVEMENT
→ [Provides]        → ◻ INSPECTABLE MOTION OR PORTRAIT PASSAGE
→ [Defines]         → ◻ MEASURED + PERCEPTUAL COMPARISON CONDITION
→ [Does Not Dictate]→ ◻ BLIND IMITATION
```

Judge every rig and face against four concrete reference bars:

1. **Anatomical & Breed Proportion Reference (Vitruvian & Age-Calibrated):**
   - Standing adult (`7.5–8.0H`, `170 cm`), Child Traveler (`5.2H`, `118 cm`), Crouched/Seated/Prone aspect-compensated bounds. A child rig must never be normalized to adult bounding-box height; a wide crouch or horizontal fall must never blow up to standing height.
2. **Joint & Seam Reference (2.5× Wrist, Ankle, Neck & Pelvis Loupe):**
   - Forearm ribbed cuffs and shin pant cuffs overlap the proximal mounting tabs of hands and boots (`Cuff-Tuck Z-Order`). The pelvis acts as a 3-socket hip girdle bridging the torso waist to both swinging thighs without exposing hollow gaps or raw cardboard triangles.
3. **Biomechanical Locomotion Reference (Karl Sims Articulated Physics):**
   - Bipedal locomotion under gravity (`1.0G` standard, `0.4G` lunar, `1.8G` heavy) driven by Central Pattern Generator (CPG) neural tendon controllers, vestibular balance reflexes, anatomical knee stops (`kneeAngle >= 0.04 rad` — never hyperextending forward), and two-point foot contact (`calcaneus heel strike` $\to$ `mid-stance plantar roll` $\to$ `Achilles toe-off`).
4. **Portrait Harmony Reference (Golden Ratio $\Phi = 1.618$ & Zero-Seam Gouache Composite):**
   - Organic, grommet-free facial cutouts feathered into blank head canvases with offscreen midtone skin harmonization (`soft-light` + `color` masked strictly via `destination-in`) so zero rectangular edges are visible at any zoom level.

### 5. BUILD BIOMECHANICS AND FACES WITH SPECIFICITY

```
◻ BIOMECHANICAL BODY
→ [Begins With]     → ◻ GRAVITY, MASS DISTRIBUTION & SUPPORT BASE
→ [Moves Through]   → ◻ ANTAGONISTIC TENDONS & LIGAMENT STOPS
→ [Contacts Ground] → ◻ VIA HEEL-STRIKE, ARCH ROLL & TOE-OFF
→ [Reveals Weight]  → ◻ THROUGH TRUNK PITCH, HIP DROP & RECOIL
```

- **Feet & Ankle Ligaments:** Never treat the foot as a single frictionless point. Model the **Calcaneus Heel Pad** and **Metatarsal Toe Pad** connected by the **Plantar Fascia Arch**, **Tibialis Anterior** (dorsiflexion during swing and heel-strike), and **Achilles Tendon** (plantarflexion push-off at late stance).
- **Knees & Cruciate/Patellar Ligaments:** Enforce strict anatomical hinge stops (`0.04 <= kneeFlex <= 1.65 rad`). A knee must never hyperextend forward like a flamingo; during swing phase, hip flexion (`+thA`) lifts the knee forward while knee flexion (`-kneeAng`) folds the shin backward.
- **Pelvis & Iliofemoral Ligaments:** The pelvis must tilt dynamically with the spine pitch and the differential swing of the left and right thighs, bridging the inguinal crease so the thighs never detach from the torso.
- **Faces & Micro-Alive Expression:** Avoid dead sticker faces and avoid chaotic random jitter. Give evolved portraits subtle respiratory drift, conjugate gaze shifts, and natural periodic blinks while preserving strict view compatibility (`profile_R` features only on `profile_R` heads).

A script exiting with code `0` is not a pass. The Critic must visually inspect the rendered contact sheets and live stage screenshots and return a structured verdict across six invariants:

1. **`INV_01 · SLOT SEMANTIC PURITY` (Measured + Judged):** Is every `torso`, `pelvis`, `thigh`, `shin`, `uarm`, `farm`, `hand`, and `foot` image actually that garment/anatomical part (zero mechanical `hipcore` triangles in outer pelvis slots, zero hands in pelvis slots, zero opera-glove leaks)?
2. **`INV_02 · VIEW & GAZE COHERENCE` (Measured):** Does `torso.profile` have a true side-profile aspect ratio (`w/h <= 0.68`), do profile shoes point forward (`+X`) along the bone vector, and do sideways/profile heads pair strictly with profile facial features?
3. **`INV_03 · CUFF-TUCK & 3-SOCKET PELVIS CONTINUITY` (Measured + Judged):** Are wrist/ankle mounting tabs tucked inside sleeve/pant cuffs, and does the pelvis bridge the hip sockets without gaps during high-knee steps and deep squats?
4. **`INV_04 · ANATOMICAL KNEE & TWO-POINT FOOT PHYSICS` (Measured):** Do knees flex strictly backward (`kneeFlex > 0`), and do stance feet exhibit heel-strike $\to$ flat roll $\to$ toe-off propulsion without moonwalking slip (`drift <= 0.6 cm/frame`)?
5. **`INV_05 · ZERO-SEAM FACE HARMONY` (Judged + Measured):** Are all facial feature composites free of rectangular alpha borders, grommet holes, and mismatched chin/beard overlaps, with $\Phi$ harmony $\ge 95\%$?

### 7. RECURSIVE INQUIRY: EVOLVE BETTER EXPERIMENTS WHEN AN AESTHETIC OR PHYSICAL GAP APPEARS

```
◻ RECURSIVE INQUIRY
→ [Observes]        → ◻ VISUAL OR BIOMECHANICAL DEFECT IN PLAYBACK
→ [Challenges]      → ◻ HIDDEN ASSUMPTION IN DATA OR SOLVER
→ [Formulates]      → ◻ DISCRIMINATING EXPERIMENT (HYPOTHESIS A vs B)
→ [Commits]         → ◻ WINNER TO CANONICAL RIG STATE
```

Whenever a character looks awkward, weightless, or misassembled, the Experiment Agent must not tweak random magic numbers. It must record and execute a **5-Step Recursive Inquiry**:

1. **Observation:** State the exact visual defect (e.g., *"Rig 4 (`woman_muse_costumes`) shows a tan triangular grommet plate between the hips and walks with her red shoes pointing backward; Rig 5 (`child_traveler_ghost`) has a giant hand over the groin"*).
2. **Current Assumption:** Identify the false assumption guiding the system (e.g., *"Assuming `kit3/v2/parts/*/body.front.pelvis.png` was correctly labeled by the legacy heuristic cutter"* or *"Assuming `globalCompositeOperation = 'soft-light'` respects destination alpha on transparent feature canvases"*).
3. **Alternative Hypothesis:** Formulate the structural cause (e.g., *"The legacy cutter sorted pieces by bounding-box Y-centroid without semantic class verification, placing `hipcore` triangles and hands into `pelvis` slots; true garment pelvises sit in `body.back.pelvis.png` or `release_v2/cuts/*__p08..p14.png`"*).
4. **Discriminating Experiment:** Build a side-by-side contact sheet and live A/B stage toggle (`CARD` vs `CODE` torso/pelvis; `1-Point Ankle` vs `2-Point Heel-Toe Rocker`; `Unmasked Soft-Light` vs `Destination-In Feathered Radial Mask`).
5. **Architectural Commit:** Promote the winning representation to the canonical catalog and add an automated invariant check so the regression can never recur.
