Puppet Kit 3ONE IMAGE · ONE PROMPT · EVERY PART A PLUG

Cineosis takes → registry.json

The next puppets for the Acting Arena: the man, the poet and Dot, rebuilt as parts that plug in. For every sheet there is one image and one prompt. Give a generator those two and it paints a full sheet of cards whose brads are already in the right place.

  1. Open the image

    Click it for full size and save it. Panel A is the layout, B is the character, C is the style.

  2. Copy the prompt

    One paragraph. It tells the model what each panel is for and what never to draw.

  3. Generate

    Paste both into the image model. The output is the painted cards on white, laid out like panel A.

  4. Drop it in

    Save the result next to its template. Its sockets are in registry.json, so there is nothing to detect.

The sheets

Character

Poison: keep these out

These sheets taught the old rig the wrong body, and they teach a generator the same. The worst is the shorts card: a pelvis piece the man does not need. It swung out like an apron and stacked four thighs in side view. Every body sheet is also the front drawing relabelled as profile, back or three-quarter. None of them goes into a pack. The packs use only the clean crops below.

Clean references

Cropped from the same sheets, keeping only cards that agree with the new build: segmented trouser legs and Chelsea boots, arm cards, hands on eyelet tabs, the head turnaround and the expression heads.

The cuts

One rest skeleton per character draws the cut charts, every template and the socket registry, so they can't disagree about where a joint is. The man has no pelvis card: his trouser thighs end in a rounded seat that tucks under the jacket hem. The poet's skirt is her only hip card.
