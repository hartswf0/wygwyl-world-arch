POEM 03 · Mirror Thread Bay

How to break off an engagement

The tambourine thrown through the window in 02 tumbles through black and sails in through a chapel's rose window, still whole, into an empty temple: empty choir stands, empty prayer lines, a dry baptism pool. In time-lapse the cobwebs are built, the pews go, the wedding gifts left at the side doors are split by lightning, bread rots in the rain and blankets hang from puppet strings with nobody on them; then the storm lifts, two sets of mud footprints walk away out of step, and the tambourine finally lands on the shore and shatters.

The chapel is dust and decay: slate darks and bone greys from the B/W carved arch 3a2ddc9d and the cathedral silhouette e0154328, rot brown (#5b4632) and dust ochre (#9a8f7a) for pews, bread and blankets. The storm and the shore take the bay's teal (#2e5f66, #8fd0d4) from fb5ef893 and 590a0c02. Lightning is the only pure white (#f2f4ee). No accent blue here: the tambourine is the subject and it stays warm wood and brass.

Insights

  • Nobody performs in the chapel: the props and time do. Budget animation for the cobwebs, the pews and the tambourine, not for puppets.
  • Things that happen over time must be built over time: a web is spun in order, prints arrive one by one, pieces land and stay landed. Never pop in a finished state.
  • The tambourine needs a skin head; as a hoop it disappears and the shatter reads as eleven lines.
  • Keep the ground three tones (water, wet sand, dry sand) and never at the figure's fill level or the walkers vanish into it.
  • The strings with nobody on them are the puppet film quoting itself: shoot them plainly, real thread, no hidden hand.
  • End with POET still walking toward the pieces; 04 starts on that trail.

For later poems

  • The tambourine pieces, the remnants shore and the trail are 04's opening set.
  • The chapel interior returns in 13 with the elders at prayer.
  • The footprint stamp is reused in 04 for both trails.
  • The cobweb and dust replacement series are reusable decay kits for any later ruin.
