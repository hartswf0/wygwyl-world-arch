POEM 03 · Mirror Thread Bay

How to break off an engagement

The tambourine thrown through the window in 02 tumbles through black and sails in through a chapel's rose window, still whole, into an empty temple: empty choir stands, empty prayer lines, a dry baptism pool. In time-lapse the cobwebs are built, the pews go, the wedding gifts left at the side doors are split by lightning, bread rots in the rain and blankets hang from puppet strings with nobody on them; then the storm lifts, two sets of mud footprints walk away out of step, and the tambourine finally lands on the shore and shatters.

The chapel is dust and decay: slate darks and bone greys from the B/W carved arch 3a2ddc9d and the cathedral silhouette e0154328, rot brown (#5b4632) and dust ochre (#9a8f7a) for pews, bread and blankets. The storm and the shore take the bay's teal (#2e5f66, #8fd0d4) from fb5ef893 and 590a0c02. Lightning is the only pure white (#f2f4ee). No accent blue here: the tambourine is the subject and it stays warm wood and brass.

The White Temple & its Pools, seen before the temple exists: this poem is the chapel the flood ruined, on the flat, under storm daylight from 10:00 to 18:30 compressed into one time-lapse. Sacred, washed, communal, but empty. The palette is slate and wet stone (#36414e, #1c262d, #44617a, #7486a0) with one warm note, the candle-and-brass gold #c99765 that belongs to the tambourine and the stained glass. Architecture as built: a small grey cantera chapel with a slate roof and short bell tower, a dark stone nave with lancets and a rose window. In cardboard the camera is locked off: every change is a card swap on a fixed plate (cobweb states, pew states, light states), never a camera move, and the macro inserts are separate large-scale cards. The chapel's old walls keep the salt line at 4/10 of their height; draw it, faint, on the nave and the exterior. No ceramic and no cyan seam yet: the white vault and its seam come later (11, 12); here we only see the stone that will be under it. Daylight is grey sky through the openings; lightning is a full-white flash card.

Staging, scene by scene

03.0 · set chapel_interior

Tambourine through the chapel window

Ode to forever— a tambourine sounds off an / empty temple,

Black belt: the tambourine card on a long rod tumbles toward camera, jingles flicking (the same card that left 02). The rose window card rises into frame from below, glowing; the tambourine passes through its centre cleanly and we are inside the nave: lancets of coloured gel either side, dust motes card, the tambourine hangs a beat in mid-air then drops out of frame.

Scene book: tracking with the tumbling tambourine in black, then a clean pass through the window into the nave. the chapel's seaward lancet

03.1 · set chapel_interior

The empty temple

empty choir stands, empty prayer lines & abandoned baptism / pools. pews taken / by cobwebs—

Locked-off plates, no camera move. Symmetrical wide down the nave: pews (3 row cards), choir stands, the dry tiled pool front centre. Time-lapse by card swaps: light slab moves across the floor, cobweb state 1-2-3, pews lose rows, ash dots drift. Macro inserts on a pew end, a kneeler and a web as separate big-scale cards.

Scene book: locked-off time-lapse plates and macro close-ups on wood, stone and web. the nave, choir loft and baptistery as built

03.2 · set chapel_side_doors

Gifts at the side doors

spirits have / left salvations in opened gift / boxes placed / outside the side doors—

Low exterior angle at threshold height: the stone step, dark side doors, three white gift boxes with lids off. Time-lapse swaps: the faint light leaving the boxes as small pale wisps rising (the spirits), the boxes sag and stain, ivy cards creep in over the cracked threshold from the left.

Scene book: low exterior angle on the threshold, time-lapse of boxes weathering. the side doors and stone threshold

03.3 · set chapel_storm

The storm

it thunders. then rains until / nothing remains.

Wide exterior from the beach: the chapel silhouette small on the flat, horizon low. Sky card swaps to blue-black; lightning fork cards flash behind, full-white flash frame on the thunder; rain dash cards on two belts at different angles. The camera pulls back (scale the plate down) as roof, tower and walls come off in pieces; the blankets on strings swing across the nave cut-away.

Scene book: wide exterior time-lapse of the chapel silhouette in lightning, pulling back as it is washed away. the flat, from the beach

03.4 · set remnants_shore

Remnants on the shore

Ode to the storm ending. what was once love / now goods— broken & sealed apart / by lightning. daily breads, / soaked & delivered rotted

Still wides on the same horizon: stumps of the walls, a pool that did not drain, the tide line strip. Inserts: soaked bread loaves, a soiled blanket in the mud, two sets of footprints walking away out of step toward the water, converging with distance.

Scene book: still wides of wreckage at the tide line, inserts on bread, blanket and mud prints. the tide line where the chapel stood

03.5 · set remnants_shore

Tambourine shatters

& a tambourine / shattered into pieces.

Ground-level, slow motion: the remnant floor strip across frame with one shaft of light on it. The tambourine card drops in from top, hits, and is replaced by the 13-piece breakaway: rim arcs, torn skin, jingles scattering toward the lens on short rods.

Scene book: slow-motion ground-level impact, fragments scattering toward lens. the chapel's remnant floor

Insights

  • Nobody performs in the chapel: the props and time do. Budget animation for the cobwebs, the pews and the tambourine, not for puppets.
  • Things that happen over time must be built over time: a web is spun in order, prints arrive one by one, pieces land and stay landed. Never pop in a finished state.
  • The tambourine needs a skin head; as a hoop it disappears and the shatter reads as eleven lines.
  • Keep the ground three tones (water, wet sand, dry sand) and never at the figure's fill level or the walkers vanish into it.
  • The strings with nobody on them are the puppet film quoting itself: shoot them plainly, real thread, no hidden hand.
  • End with POET still walking toward the pieces; 04 starts on that trail.
  • Atlas: the chapel is small, grey and alone on a flat with a mirror of wet sand under it; the emptiness of the horizon is the set. Keep the horizon low and the chapel small in every exterior.
  • Atlas: the halfworld frames already draw the poem's breakaways as flat graphics (rose window into wedge petals, blankets hanging on straight strings cut by a lightning crack). Cut the shard and string cards from those shapes.
  • Atlas: the chapel is under the line: draw the faint salt line at 4/10 on its walls; there is no white ceramic yet, that belongs to the temple built over it later.

For later poems

  • The tambourine pieces, the remnants shore and the trail are 04's opening set.
  • The chapel interior returns in 13 with the elders at prayer.
  • The footprint stamp is reused in 04 for both trails.
  • The cobweb and dust replacement series are reusable decay kits for any later ruin.
