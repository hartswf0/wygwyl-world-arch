POEM 07 · Naraka Market

DJ Turn Me Up

A full house party in an Atlantis house: the DJ drops onto the decks and scratches, and the poet, standing at the bar as in the part 1 reading video, eyes shut and chin pressed down under the weight of his words, tells the love story while a projection wall behind him shows it: ghosts walking out of a dying vineyard, two wedding bands drifting apart, a house trucked off down one-way streets, eleven petals plucked off a daisy, three roads. The whole poem is one climb in volume: starved at the start, turnt up at the end, chin high, the room erupting.

Night interior #0b0f1e and the district cyan #01acd4 from Naraka Market (22ddf809 blue vapour, e3c427ae teal market crowd). Magenta #e0479e and amber #ffb04a from the neon container fronts (b631fc80, 79ea20ae) and the pink-orange haze of the crowd blur a883ad75. Haze violet #8a6fa8 is that same smoke. Vineyard green #6e8b3d fading to dry straw #c9b37a for the projected vineyard. Silver #cfd6e0 is the one accent, the silver lining in M6 (world module: level 8 spent exactly once).

Naraka Market humid, crowded, devotional, improvised

hourkept at twilight by its own light even at 1 a.m. (06 streets, 07 house)
weatherlight rain, steam, teal haze
lightthe Relighting: neon and string lights, candles in the alleys; teal and pink
architectureshipping containers stacked two to four high into shops and rooms; corrugated awnings; cable webs and string lights; hidden stairwells between walls; the underpass flooded into a shadow-pool; the teahouse
cameraSteadicam behind him down alleys two people wide; candle and window close-ups; the crowd at chest height
soundmany radios, a kettle, the DJ through the walls, rain on corrugated roofs
design languagerust plated in ceramic, seams drawn by Glaziers at every container door; the salt line is chalked here, not painted (Night of the Line)

07 never leaves Naraka Market: Atlantis from below, a twilight the market keeps by its own light even at 1:30 a.m. The House is a casona built into stacked shipping containers, so every wall card is corrugated steel (score vertical ribs into the card and dry-brush teal #337993 / rust #5f463b), with string lights as pin-pricks, cable webs as black thread, and white ceramic plates with a cyan Glazier seam where the rust broke; the projection wall is a container's rusted side, and the projections should show the corrugation through them. Light is the Relighting palette: teal haze, magenta and amber neon, the DJ's LED piping. Code the haze, the meter and the strobe; paint everything else. The salt line is chalked on the container at 4 m. Never show a window to the sky: the only way out of the room is the back stair to the red shaft.

Hero buildings

The House

the whole poem: front room, bar, DJ booth on its landing, projection wall, the floor under the dark cloud

Build as cards: a three-wall box set of corrugated container cards (scored ribs, teal/rust dry-brush), one wall with a white ceramic plate and cyan seam; the projection wall is a separate rust card with a slot behind for projection prints; the booth on a 6 cm riser by the back stair; ceiling card pierced with star holes over a light box (holes closed by sliding black tabs); bar counter with neon underglow strip. Nothing breaks; the room changes only by light and scale.

The Spire Elevator Shaft

the exit: after the room erupts he takes the back stair to the open cage and rides the red shaft up to the rooftop club of 08

Build as cards: looking up: 8-10 nested square frame cards with cross-bracing cut in, spaced on rods and lit from the side with red gel so each floor rakes; a landing lamp every frame; the cage is a wire-mesh box card that rises past them. The doors at the top are two sliding cards that open on violet.

Insights

  • Amplitude is the whole design: one envelope scales the poet, the mic and the central object of each movement, anchored at a fixed stage floor. In cut-out, print the poet's cards at two or three scales or scale the rig in comp.
  • Put the love story on the projection wall, not in the room: it keeps the poet at the bar (the treatment's source, Mark at the bar) and lets the images be flat prints, which cut-out does best.
  • Make the middle movements move: each has one quantity running the whole way (sky emptying, gap opening, petals counting down) and an arrival at the end; avoid holding still pictures for 14 s.
  • Reaction shots are the treatment's ask: give guests real faces and hands (hand on chest, lighter up, mouthing along).
  • The EX appears only as a projected ghost, back to us; this keeps the rule that his face is never seen.
  • Spend the silver accent only on the lining in M6.
  • Atlas: the House is built into shipping containers; score the wall cards as corrugated steel and let the projections run over the ribs. The texture tells the audience they are in Naraka, not a generic club.
  • Atlas: foundry/07-02 already draws the three roads (rain, fog, silver-lined cloud) as flat line panels; it is the triptych, print it.
  • Atlas: the projected ghosts work better as back-lit silhouettes on tracing paper (foundry/07-01) than as superimposition; it is native to cut-out and keeps the EX's face unseen by construction.

For later poems

  • The poet's chin-high pose and the open mic are the 'one open mic at a time' that leads into 08 (he leaves the party for the rooftop).
  • The house and its crowd are reused for the 14 'hot minute' ending and the vintage party echoes of 01.
  • The three-roads panel feeds 10's ride out of Atlantis.
  • The daisy is a small reusable prop for any later love-counting beat (13 How To Win My Heart).
