POEM 14 · The Ascent

Hot Minute

The finale. Haze gathers on the water and climbs, and everything the poet built, the city, the temple, the harbor, becomes weather; then it takes him last, from the feet up, while he resists. He floats again in the black of 01, but there are no walls this time, and his life flashes past as a reel of the suite's own motifs. Two of him dance and land the spin as a victory, and an old vintage door appears in the black. He opens it on a slow-motion party, the kind he once fell out of a window to escape, walks into the crowd, and the door closes.

Ascent blue #2560e1 (the poem's colour in film/poems.json) is the icy aerial Atlantis of 434b7bdd_1; the haze is blue-grey #8fa6b8 rising to near-white #d7dde2, taken from the fogged observation deck in 993cc7a2_0, not the orange of the haze district (orange is saved). The void is #050507 with a faint open horizon #1a1d2b. The single accent the film spends is the warm light under and through the door, #ffc46b; inside, the vintage party is amber #b5652e and wine #7a1f2b (a883ad75_3's warm blur, 01's party).

Two districts and no geography. The Orange Haze (14.0) is low-pressure sodium: one orange wavelength that greys every other colour, so the harbour set is repainted in a single orange ramp from #fa8d15 down to #351106 and everything that is not orange reads grey. Silhouettes are small on catwalks and platforms over nothing; never a close-up outside. The haze is built as stacked translucent cards rising from the ground. Then The Black (14.1-14.6): no line, no seam, no floor, no walls. A black stage with the figure lit alone by one coded top light; the only architecture is the door. Its only colours are the flashing lights and then the warm amber leak of the vintage party under and around the door. Never draw a horizon line in the black except the one faint noise-bent strip the schema already has; never show the flood line or a cyan seam there.

Insights

  • Use the same haze function for the city and for him: 'I have been weather before' only holds if it is literally the same mechanism.
  • Keep orange out of the haze; the warm door light is the one accent the film spends, and it lands harder if the whole poem before it is blue-grey and black.
  • NO WALLS is 01's black without the room: no rectangle anywhere, one open horizon, so the audience feels the difference from the opening poem.
  • The reel is the suite's own motifs and nothing else, each icon stopping at the noun; reuse the props built for 01-13 as flat insert cards rather than drawing new ones.
  • He exits through a door he chooses, not a window he falls through: keep the shut window visible in the party to say so.
  • Transcript correction from the treatment: 'twisted dons' is 'twisted dawns'. The reading departs from the treatment text elsewhere ('sharpened key of answers', 'gray tones into our melanin'); set the reading.
  • Atlas: 14 has no foundry frames; the archive's door-of-light (archive/925) and the red doors at the corridor end (views/006) carry the door beat. Build the door's light as a coded spill that pours toward camera along the floor ring, not a glow around the frame.
  • Atlas: re-grade the harbour wide to one orange ramp before the haze climbs; sodium light greys everything else, so the white temple goes grey last.
  • Atlas: the three figures on the catwalk are the same three cards in the party crowd; reuse them deliberately so the loop is legible.
  • Atlas: the Black has no line and no seam; keep every city detail (flood line, cyan seam, tide boards) out of 14.1-14.6.

For later poems

  • This is the end. The door closing is the last image of the suite; the party and the door return the film to 01's slow party, so both poems should share the same party kit, garland and window.
  • The reel strip can serve as a title or credits device for the whole film.
