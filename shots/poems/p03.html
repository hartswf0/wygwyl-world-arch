🏛️ Stage Sets & Places (5)

Die-cut multiplane environments and cardboard scenic layers.

black_void

black space the tambourine tumbles through after leaving the living-room window (continues 02)

chapel_interior

empty stone chapel: rose window high on the west wall, empty choir stands, empty prayer lines (kneelers), wooden pews, abandoned dry baptism pool; dressed in three time-lapse states (intact, cobwebbed, rotting)

chapel_side_doors

outside the chapel's side doors: stone step, opened gift boxes left on it

chapel_storm

the chapel from outside and in during a heavy storm: lightning, driving rain, roof failing, floodwater carrying goods out

remnants_shore

after the storm: chapel remnants (pew ends, rose-window lead, stones) strewn on a mud shore; a pool that did not drain; two trails of footprints leading inland
