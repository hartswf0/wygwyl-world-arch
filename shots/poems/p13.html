Movement SMALL TABLES (13s)

Scene 13.1

"I'd want you to notice me. / in a corner cafe— / at the harbor / of the heavens / wandering in thoughts ... (Find me at the harbor, at the small tables, watching.)"

Staging Treatment: Daytime/sunlight. POET wanders from new temple around harbor to café. At the café, POET observes romantic interests at other tables.

Shot Framing / Cam Set Action Puppet Bodies Props
13.1a wide
eye		 harbor_cafe_day daytime: POET leaves the white temple and wanders the harbor path toward the café; the tide is out, boats aground POET (red_black_sunglasses): threequarter · walk · wanders along the quay, head turned to the water moored_boat
13.1b full
tracking		 harbor_cafe_day POET walks the length of the quay past the small tables while the tide comes in under them and the near boat rights itself POET (red_black_sunglasses): profile · walk · walks to the third table cafe_table, moored_boat
13.1c medium
eye		 harbor_cafe_day POET lowers himself onto the third chair and sits watching: his head keeps going out to the water and to the couples at the other tables POET (red_black_sunglasses): threequarter · sit · sits, watching the other tables
