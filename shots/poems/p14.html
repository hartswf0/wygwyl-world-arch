Movement EVERYTHING I BUILT (13s)

Scene 14.1

"(won't prevent me / from sharing / a smile worth / the promise of my heart.) A hot minute. The haze gathers on the water — and everything I built here learns to be weather: the city, the temple, the harbor."

Staging Treatment: Haze gathers on water, over harbor, and grows. Atlantis, the new temple and the harbor all slowly become haze.

Movement FROM THE GROUND UP (13s)

Scene 14.2

"Don't make me leave / & go where / promises— ... (It takes me last, and from the ground up. I am reluctant — but I have been weather before.)"

Staging Treatment: Haze slowly envelopes POET from ground up body, who is reluctant at first. [reference: Lawrence Raab, "Walking Alone"]

Shot Framing / Cam Set Action Puppet Bodies Props
14.2a full

14.2b close

14.2c full

Scene 14.3

Staging Treatment: POET is floating again in all black.

Movement THE REEL (15s)

Scene 14.4

"absent artificial escapes / & machine learned / strengths / from our highs / & lows / as sourced materials— ... (A life flashes the way a reel does: the window, the tambourine, the field, the stars, the candle, the daisy, the ride, the temple, the hourglass.)"

Staging Treatment: Cut back and forth from POET floating to B-Roll scenes of life. Human scenes that capture the highs and lows of life. Cut back and forth from POET floating in blackness.
