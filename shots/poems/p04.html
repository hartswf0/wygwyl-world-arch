Movement WASHED ASHORE (13s)

Scene 04.1

"What if i followed this / trail to where the / broken pieces have / washed ashore— / & the ashes of / expired wildfires / cover sands & debris / lifted from the night sea / water's floor."

Staging Treatment: Cut to POET (now in dressed in Red/Black outfit with sunglasses), standing on shore at night/twilight. POET's hands are in pockets. POET has no heart. Poet walks along looking at decay, remnants, shattered pieces (never see hands, always in pockets), ravens flying overhead.

Shot Framing / Cam Set Action Puppet Bodies Props
04.1a wide
eye		 shore_night cut to POET standing on the ash-covered shore at twilight, hands in pockets, chapel remnants and debris around him, ravens wheeling overhead POET (red_black_sunglasses): front · stand · stands still, taking in the wreckage tambourine_pieces, chapel_remnants, ravens
04.1b full
tracking		 shore_night POET walks the trail of shattered tambourine pieces, head bowed to read it, hands never leaving his pockets POET (red_black_sunglasses): profile · walk · walks slowly, head down, reading the trail tambourine_pieces, sea_debris
04.1c medium
eye		 shore_night POET's chest: an empty hollow where the heart should be, the night visible through it POET (red_black_sunglasses): front · stand · stops; the hollow in his chest catches the moonlight —

Movement FOLDED WHISPERS (14s)

Scene 04.2

"the waves will / calm to folded whispers / & the winds / will mute the trees / for the right / level of silence / to find a throbbing heart / left at the midlife / corner we turn toward / the incline— / a familiar heart / which has shaken / off every / sad blue note / life can perform / to overcome / & spread inspiration / & it used to be mine. / I'll lift it with care, / putting it back in place. / & tell myself / nevermore—"

Staging Treatment: POET finds heart on shore, without using hands (still in pockets), heart floats and reenters POET.

Shot Framing / Cam Set Action Puppet Bodies Props
04.2a wide
eye		 shore_night the waves calm to folded whispers, the trees go still; POET kneels by a half-buried tambourine rim where something throbs POET (red_black_sunglasses): threequarter · kneel · kneels at the pile, head bowed, hands still in pockets tambourine_pieces, heart
04.2b insert
eye		 shore_night the heart in the sand, beating, shaking off grit heart
04.2c medium
eye		 shore_night without his hands the heart lifts by itself, floats slowly across to him and re-enters his chest; he says nevermore POET (red_black_sunglasses): profile · kneel · straightens as the heart slides into his chest heart

Movement MUSTARD SEEDS (13s)

Scene 04.3

"What if i followed this / reclaimed heart to where / the puzzle pieces / of faith are handcrafted / scrambled & scattered as / mustard seeds / into the vast moonlit / night's / garden fields."

Staging Treatment: POET sees trail and follows it up to field, ravens flying overhead. Walking through field as described, (still hands in pockets).

04.3c full

Staging Treatment: Walking through field as described, (still hands in pockets).

Movement I PUT THEM BACK ON (14s)

Scene 04.6

"hands that once / could touch dust after dusk / find & celebrate a new / life & feel good— / & they too / used to be mine. / I put them / back on with / the care of my future / at stake / & say to myself— / nevermore. / I have won at the world / & lost my soul— / I want nothing more / that to just be / myself— / & nothing more / & be alright, / I do believe / I'll be alright. / this reclaimed / & dusk settled heart; / rediscovered but dusted / hands— / remind me, / but say to me in this / still muted tree / & night garden field / silence— / nevermore. / Now go."

Staging Treatment: POET finds hands, put them back on, and stands in field reflecting (as described).
