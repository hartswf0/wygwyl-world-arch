POEM 01 · Naraka Market

Out of Life

A dim high-rise bedroom where a man reads a poem to a back that will not turn around: the EX on the fire escape, vaping, while the haze eats the room left to right and the window slides away from every step toward it. Then two bodies fall past streaking towers with a gap between them that breathes and never closes, through a slow-motion vintage party and a hole in its floor, until the only light left in the film is the vape ember, a blue speck on black.

Night blacks and wet teal come from Naraka stills 22ddf809 (blue vapour bloom over a lit shopfront) and 62b9740c (flooded tunnel, cold light at the end); the steel greys and lit window specks from the hive fall shot 8519865f. The warm amber/gold pair (#c9873a, #e9b46a) is the vintage party's light leaks (a883ad75) and the bedroom's one lamp. #5aa7ff is the suite accent and in this poem it belongs to the vape ember alone: the last light in the film. #2a1a2e is the plum of the party's darks so the ballroom does not go brown.

Two districts, one hard cut. The Orange Haze (scenes 01.0-01.2) is the New Boroughs under low-pressure sodium: one orange wavelength that greys every other colour. In cardboard that means the OUTSIDE of the window is painted in a single orange ramp (#fa8d15 down to #351106) with no other hue at all; brick, iron, planters and figures are only darker steps of the same orange, and the haze is opaque painted card that replaces what it covers, never a dimmer. Inside the bedroom keep the dossier's cold teal-blue TV light so the window is the one warm hole in a cold room; the two must never mix on the same card. Architecture is iron hung on wet brick: switchback fire escapes, a spiral stair with planters in a light well, a catwalk with a glowing kerb far below. The salt line sits at 4/10 of every rail as a pale chalk stroke; cyan seams are rare here and read as alarms, so the only cyan allowed is the vape ember. Never show a close-up outside; outside figures are small silhouettes. The Black (01.3-01.5) has no geography and no line or seam: a black stage, the figure lit alone, and the only architecture is the party's warm leak and the hole in its floor.

Insights

  • The EX works because he is a back: one back-of-head card, the bomber's silhouette and the ember carry him. Do not build him a face.
  • The haze must replace, not dim: paint it opaque and reveal it in a sweeping mask, the way the halfworld swaps dot allegiance. A cross-fade reads as a lighting cue, not a substance.
  • Put the window on a slider. The single gag of the poem is a destination that recedes; it is cheaper and stronger than any camera move.
  • In the fall keep the towers at mid value and the bodies at full ink; when the towers had full contrast the fall became wallpaper.
  • Shoot the party extras soft and on 4s and POET hard and on 2s: the slow motion is the difference between them.
  • End on the ember alone for a held beat; it is the hand-off to 02's flashing lights.
  • Atlas: the outside of the window is the Orange Haze under sodium light: paint it in one orange ramp only, and keep the bedroom's teal/blue inside. The window is the warm hole in a cold room; never let teal haze spill outside or orange inside.
  • Atlas: the halfworld frame foundry/01-04 is already the bedroom's layout (drawers left, door at the vanishing point, cross-mullion window right). Build the set from it rather than from the photographic stills.
  • Atlas: build the vintage party door card once; 14 closes on it and 01 opens on it, so the film loops through one shared card.

For later poems

  • The black-tshirt POET pack and his falling cards serve 02 (floating in the black void).
  • The EX's back-only pack, now proven, carries him through 02, 03, 07, 08, 09.
  • The tower-strip belt returns for 02's road and 05's ascent.
  • The vintage party set and couples come back in 14.
  • The blue ember is the first appearance of the suite accent; 04 gives it to the heart.
