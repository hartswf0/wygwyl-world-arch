Movement SILENT SCREAM (13s)

Scene 02.1

"I heard a / scream for help / while falling / with my / headlights / off— / it was / the type of silent scream / a trapped lover makes to / escape / a pending marriage, / a concussion, / a set of walls caving inward / — / seething & simmering / in a solitude of / crowds & smiles."

Staging Treatment: POET in black space falling or floating, lights flashing. EX in distress, also floating unreachable.

Shot Framing / Cam Set Action Puppet Bodies Props
02.1a wide
eye		 black_void POET floats in black space as light flashes sweep across; far off EX floats in distress, back to us, out of reach POET (black_tshirt): threequarter · float · drifts, turning toward a scream he can't place
EX (ex_jacket): back · float · writhes in distress, arms thrown out, unreachable		 —
02.1b close
low		 black_void the silent scream: POET's head thrown back, mouth open with no sound, hands clawing up as the walls cave in POET (black_tshirt): front · float · screams silently, body tightening as the walls close —
02.1c insert
eye		 black_void two engagement rings drift toward each other in the dark and never touch engagement_rings

Movement THE MIRROR (12s)

Scene 02.2

"I receive an / emergency call / while tossing & turning— / it was / the type of silent call / a man makes to / the mirror after midnight / to digest a truth,"

Staging Treatment: (treatment silent; staged from the line) Everything still black.

Shot Framing / Cam Set Action Puppet Bodies Props
02.2a wide
top_down		 bedroom_night POET tosses in bed in the dark; the phone on the nightstand lights and buzzes twice POET (black_tshirt): profile · lie · tosses and turns under the sheet phone
02.2b full
eye		 bedroom_night POET sits up, gets out of bed and crosses the dark floor to the mirror POET (black_tshirt): threequarter_back · walk · crosses to the mirror, head low —
02.2c medium
eye		 bedroom_night at the glass: POET puts a hand flat on the mirror and his reflection's hand meets it exactly POET (black_tshirt): profile · stand · palm flat to the mirror, looking himself in the eye mirror

Movement WE GO THROUGH (13s)

Scene 02.3

"a winless insomnia, / an epiphany of a road / winding down— / after a day of life / dilated at the sun / as long & as wise / as possible. / We won't make it"

Staging Treatment: Everything still black, eyes dilate trying to see in the dark. Going into eyes, through eyes, we are in a flashback.

Shot Framing / Cam Set Action Puppet Bodies Props
02.3a close
eye		 black_void POET's face barely lit in the black, straining to see POET (black_tshirt): front · stand · stares into the dark, unblinking —
02.3b extreme_close
eye		 eye_macro the pupil dilates; inside it a night road winds down to one far light —
02.3c pov
tracking		 driveway_road_night we go through the pupil and come out above the childhood driveway: a car's headlights crest the bumps and swing toward the house family_car

Movement THE GHOST WAS SEVEN (14s)

Scene 02.4

"I was six— / I recall, when / the ghost of a dead / & a love-less / seven-year old child / appeared & shared / that my parents' / marriage wouldn't / last— / It was the type of / warm & heavy breeze / in winter where / a child is left / home alone"

Staging Treatment: Young POET at living room window watching car pull up, lights flashing through room as they shine in because of light angle and bumps in the road. Ghost kid appears and hugs Young POET. Ghost disappears, Young POET still at living room window, watching parents argue in the car.

Shot Framing / Cam Set Action Puppet Bodies Props
02.4a wide
eye		 living_room_1990s YOUNG_POET (six) stands at the living-room window, back to us, as headlights sweep across the walls and the door post's height marks YOUNG_POET (pajamas_winter): back · stand · watches the car pull up, nose near the glass height_marks, wedding_rings
02.4b full
eye		 living_room_1990s the ghost child, one notch taller, fades in beside him and hugs him from the side; she does not breathe YOUNG_POET (pajamas_winter): profile · stand · stiffens, then leans into the hug
GHOST_CHILD (ghost_nightclothes): profile · stand · wraps both arms around him and whispers that the marriage won't last		 —
02.4c medium
through_window		 driveway_road_night over YOUNG_POET's shoulder (the ghost gone): his parents argue inside the idling car, silhouettes lit by the dash YOUNG_POET (pajamas_winter): back · stand · alone again at the glass, hand on the sill
PARENT_1 (parent_coat): profile · sit · argues, hands slicing the air
PARENT_2 (parent_coat): profile · sit · shouts back, turned away to the side window		 family_car, steering_wheel

"& no tears / & no fucks / & no love / to give. / ode to teach."
