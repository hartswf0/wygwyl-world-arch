Movement PULSING (13s)

Scene 08.1

"Pulsing & head throbbing from the music— / & empty on a crowded floor of sweated perfumes & temptations."

Staging Treatment: [style: time-shifted photo-realistic] [video inspo: Madonna, Ray of Light; Janet Jackson, Got 'Til It's Gone] Leaves party/poetry-reading goes to open-air, rooftop intense club scene. Dancing is fast, lights are flashing, people are enjoying themselves, time is shifted. (p.17)

DANCER_1 (club_dress): front · dance · dances fast, arms up

DANCER_1 (club_dress): threequarter · dance · dances past him

Movement THE ESCAPE (14s)

Scene 08.2

"The soul has escaped my body for the moment / & left him on the dance floor— / He can now just be flesh & bones— / rejection & heartache hit different / when there are no feelings afloat."

Staging Treatment: Soul of POET leaves body and floats above. (p.17, beside these lines)

Movement IMAGINE MY BODY (13s)

Scene 08.3

"I want my soul now to imagine my body. / Let's watch strangers collide & spark electric currents that gamut the mood— a good one. / We want him to be the last one to leave, / prolonging the bitter refraction from the escape— / inside the walls of an escape that few pleasures offer us."

Staging Treatment: Cut away to POET's Soul watching party floating from afar, top-down of all Atlantis, lit up, blue and pink lights throughout the city. (p.18, beside 'bitter refraction from the escape— inside the walls of an escape')

Movement THE EQUATOR (13s)

Scene 08.4

"Now let us put my soul in the middle of an open field— / at the equator of a planet much larger than my own— / & much hotter."

Staging Treatment: No note on the planet itself; staged from the line. The treatment's next note sits beside these lines (p.18): 'Cut back to body in club, angels dumping cleansing water on POET's Body in the middle of the club scene.' — set up here, paid off in 08.5.

Scene 08.5

"Cool me down by pouring unfiltered water / out of ice baths—salted from the closest moons / from angles of the newest religions / & its joys capturing my body trancing in a flash of strobe lights. / their graces curse or bless me— / only infinity will tell."

Staging Treatment: Cut back to body in club, angels dumping cleansing water on POET's Body in the middle of the club scene. (p.18)

Staging Treatment: Club fills with haze, POET's Body inhales it as POET's soul returns. (p.19)

Movement MOUNTAINS IN THE DISTANCE (13s)

Scene 08.7

"Staring into unfamiliar yet—relatable mountains in the distance / & wandering eyes & wandering thoughts within reach. / [Yet, Heard:] accepting you'll never say nor ever feel my love was real— / what isn't mine, I still can not give."

Staging Treatment: POET stares into mountains from rooftop. (p.19) Then, no voice over [inspo video: Juice Jones, The Rain]: POET leaves clubs, walks down street. Crowds on the sidewalk, as men and woman fawn over POET. POET sees EX through crowd (still back of EX, no face shown) and tries to reach him but cannot. (p.19; the last two lines are printed under Yet, Heard but belong to this movement in the world module)
