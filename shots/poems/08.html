POEM 08 · Ankhu Spires

Newly Single

He leaves the party for an open-air rooftop club over Atlantis, all strobes and blue-pink light, where the dancers smear in shifted time and he stands still and empty. His soul, a glowing see-through copy of him, peels out and floats up, watches the whole city lit below, is set down on a white-hot plain at the equator of a giant planet, and is cooled by angels pouring ice baths; back in the club the same water lands on his body under the strobe. Haze fills the room, the soul comes home, he leans back and breathes it in, and ends on the ledge staring at far mountains, then walks out into a street where he glimpses the EX's back and cannot reach him.

Insights

  • Never merge the two copies: even after the return keep both cards in frame, the soul printed on acetate over the body. The line never says they became one thing again.
  • Time shift is a frame-rate contrast: dancers on 2s and 3s with ghost doubles, the poet on 1s. Cheap in cut-out, and it reads instantly.
  • 'Much hotter' is a subtraction: blow the sky away until the plain stands in white; do not add flames.
  • Intercut body and soul on the pour: the same water lands on both, which is how the poem's 'cool me down' connects two places.
  • The planet is a floor: a horizon so wide it barely curves; one red-white ground card and ticks, nothing scattered on it (open means empty).
  • Strobes: keep flashes under safe rates; let the haze and the colour carry the club instead.
  • The last stanza and the street beat bleed into 09 (Yet Heard): the EX's back through the crowd is the hinge.

For later poems

  • The soul acetate set returns wherever the poet is beside himself (09, 11).
  • The rooftop and club crowd (club_dress/shirt/jacket) are reused in 09.
  • The night street with the EX walking away opens 09; the mountains seen from the rooftop are the horizon he rides toward in 10.
  • The top-down Atlantis print is reused for 10's dusk exit and 14's ending.
  • The angels can return in 11 New Day as a blessing image.
