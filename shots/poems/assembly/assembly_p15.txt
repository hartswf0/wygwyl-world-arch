[style: photo-realistic cyberpunk]
[source material: part 1 poetry reading
video; specifically Mark standing at bar.]

Dj turn me up, please.
With eyes wide shut
& my chin nested at the
arch
of the weight of these
spoken words—
I have a love story to tell…
of
ghosts—
whispering unfolded
dilutions,
birthed in purity
& dying in the vineyards
of sunsoaked,
evergreen fields
unnourished.
a love story
of
one by one,
treasures escaping.
& devaluing
themselves after defamed
unsullied stars
diminished their glares—
&
i walked 1,000 light years,
across the
greatest divide
to be heard—so

DJ is dropped into the house and
spins/scratches.
POET gives neo-soul inspired performance
at full house party—reaction shots, crowd
interacting, as described.
