Tambourine falling, flying, tumbling through
black space.
Ode to forever—
a tambourine sounds off an
empty temple,
empty choir stands,
empty prayer lines
& abandoned baptism
pools.

Tambourine files into chapel through
window, still whole.
[style: time-lapse photo-realistic]

Chapel decay, close-ups.

pews taken
by cobwebs—
& doused in
disintegration
spirits have
left salvations
in opened gift
boxes placed
outside the side doors—
it thunders.
then rains until
nothing remains.
Ode to the storm ending.
what was once love
now goods—
broken & sealed apart
by lightning.
daily breads,
soaked & delivered rotted
by rain—

Heavy storm takes over, speeds decay.

Storm ends, remnants of chapel on shore.
