Decades later—there is
less time for words
& more space for laughter,
hugs with no reason—
loud voices so our
hearts can be
overheard.
My mind drifts into
the darkness of our
afterlives
& those rooms are empty.
We are all here—
each holding hour glasses
of burning sands
& known journeys
& grateful to every living
tree
in the forest for the
shared airs
& the shared memories.
This is brotherhood.
smiles with no reason—
narrowed differences so
our
hearts can be
overheard.
Decades later—
we can place our ears
upon the next brother’s
soul
& follow paths
of hilltops & canyons
of blinding lights
& infinite nights
