Movement MORNING FOG (13s)

Scene 11.1

"river-rafting fog / covers the seas in / the distance— / & hugs the bases of / tree covered hills— / this world is / at peace."

Staging Treatment: POET watches fog/haze drifts in off of undeveloped shoreline.

Movement FIRST COURSES (13s)

Scene 11.2

"In silent resounds— / choir chords / calm the nerves / of things I can't control / & offer them / as keepsakes back to the / higher powers. / Exhausted emotions / carry forward / a diminishing flame / to emit jazz-tuned / lyrics / that heat / & dry / the morning dew / on the remnants of / the destruction / revealed / in the sunlight."

Staging Treatment: POET begins to rebuild a new temple, or rather it builds itself with POET. Temple is not old, but futuristic, modern, clean and white.

Shot Framing / Cam Set Action Puppet Bodies Props
11.2a wide
eye		 temple_shore Dawn comes up from the horizon; POET walks in among the chapel rubble, still dark in the valley, and stops where the platform will be POET (red_black_sunglasses): profile · walk · walks onto the site chapel_rubble
11.2b medium
low		 temple_shore POET lifts both palms and the first white course of the platform arrives under them, dot by dot, and settles POET (red_black_sunglasses): threequarter · stand · raises his palms; the stone comes with them temple
11.2c wide
eye		 temple_shore Three courses down; POET raises his arms on the struck note as the third settles; the sun dries the dew off the remnants of the chapel POET (red_black_sunglasses): front · stand · raises both arms temple

Movement COLUMNS AND BEAMS (14s)

Scene 11.3

Staging Treatment: (Continues: the temple builds itself with POET.) Staged from the line: under sun rays, columns and beams go up.

Staging Treatment: (Treatment silent; staged from the module line: laps around infinity pools of edgeless dreams that reflect the enormity of the skies; the journal from the poem line.)

Scene 11.5

"when words / are read aloud— / & sung alone as / gospels to the lonely / mirror's / midnight hours— / that left tears / drying with time / that has come / & too passed—"

Staging Treatment: (Treatment silent; staged from the module line: that we too can walk on water; deep breaths.)

Movement A NEW DAY (14s)

Scene 11.6

"We can embrace a new day; / We can receive a new day; / We can welcome a new day. / Everything / I've known & / learned / every / circumference of life / worth celebrating / & believing / are here / together, in / this sacred place / reveling / in the sunlight / of a new beginning / with this world— / & a heart, / at peace."

Staging Treatment: Temple stands with POET built on shoreline as described.
