Movement THE SHARED MOON (17s)

Scene 09.1

"accepting you'll never / say nor / ever feel / my love was real— / what isn't mine, / I still can not give. / I gather my things to go. / before I go— / we look at the moon— / the way we used to see it / & settle down the stoicism. / we talk for years / & travel through time / & it feels so good— / we grow old together / I believe, maybe / we could have it all— / swinging my humbled / late nights & / heartbreaks at / an end story / unchanging."

Staging Treatment: No voice over: POET leaves clubs, walks down street. Crowds on the sidewalk, as men and woman fawn over POET. POET sees EX through crowd (still back of EX, no face shown) and tries to reach him but cannot. / POET and EX walking down Atlantis street together, talking, moon in distance. (Aging shouldn't be literal in visuals.)

Movement TEARS, AN ABYSS (18s)

Scene 09.2

"You help me / gather my things to go. / before I go— / I call on my mother. / In tears— / speaking of an abyss / she's never known. / & we talk for years / & re-closet wounds / & re-explain my way of birth / then & even after prayer / what isn't mine, / I still can not give. / we grow old together / with the moon looking / at us—the way / we used to be / as my tears fall between / the creases in / faith— / dancing on my own / maternal ears in tune."

Staging Treatment: EX and POET are standing on sidewalk near phone booth with suitcases at their feet. POET calls MOTHER (enters phonebooth?). POET and MOTHER are talking while watching POET and EX break-up on sidewalk in background. Cut to POET and MOTHER in living room talking with POET and EX breaking up in background.

Movement HIS FOOTSTEPS, RETRACED (18s)

Scene 09.3

"She watches us / gather my things to go. / before I go— / I call on my Father / summon his calming / demeanor / from the yonders / & we walk for years / & travel east toward the sunrises / & retrace his footsteps / now & then. / guiding a settling down / moon—the way / it used to leave / & my tears fall between / the creases in wave / shadows / of the inner harbor—"

Staging Treatment: POET hangs up, MOTHER disappears. POET calls FATHER, FATHER appears and watches POET and EX break up on sidewalk. Cut to Poet and FATHER walking down paths as described with POET and EX breaking up in background.

Movement EAST, AT THE HARBOR (16s)

Scene 09.4

"We stroll, / Father & son, / hearted & shaken / Someone borrowed / & someone blue / yet, broken— / yet, heard."

Staging Treatment: POET and EX on sidewalk again, EX turns to haze and dissapears. (Stroll staged from the line: father and son walk east as the sun comes up out of the harbor mouth.)
