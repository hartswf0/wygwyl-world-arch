POEM 10 · The Orange Haze

Magic Ride

Alone on the sidewalk after the breakup, the poet finds a motorcycle he wasn't looking for, rubs his aggressions into its steel and rides. The bike holds frame centre and the world streams past it: neon Atlantis, rows of heads that turn, the city's last towers, then mountains, while the night is replaced by morning one dot at a time. He arrives, chilled and innocent, at a ruined chapel on the shore.

Night is deep violet-black and indigo (ba11d121 skyline, night streets); the city's light is sodium amber and orange haze from the district stills (c8158a76 glowing road edges, 5bd8dc26 neon slashes, 31ca5420 smog skyline). Morning is paper cream with a soft gold sun; the last movement cools to a chilly pale blue. The single accent is the white glint off the steel tank, lit by moon, then its own spark, then sun.

Insights

  • Never move the bike: one fixed camera, everything streams past. It costs strips, not animation.
  • Two builds of the motorcycle: a solid machine only for the one close shot, a skeleton for the ride.
  • Put the poet behind the machine at the mount, or the two silhouettes merge.
  • Head turns are loose head discs nudged sideways, not rotated bodies.
  • The dawn is a hard ragged edge pulled across the sky, not a fade: in cut-out it is literally one card slid over another.
  • Keep motion smear tight; wide smear turns the crowd into ghosts.

For later poems

  • The motorcycle stays parked at the chapel in 11.
  • The chapel ruin and the cold shore are 11's opening set.
  • The moon disc closes its run here; the sun disc carries into 11.
  • Streaming strips for the Atlantis skyline can serve any later travel shot.
