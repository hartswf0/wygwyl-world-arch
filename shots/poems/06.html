POEM 06 · The Light Gate & Atlantis Central Plaza

Resurrecting Atlantis

Generations walk a yellow-brick causeway out over black water toward a drowned city whose spire tips barely break the surface, while comets carry souls to the capital dome. The waterline falls once across the whole poem and the city comes up streaming, neon and vines on wet marble; the poet pays a glowing coin at the admission booth, walks candlelit narrow streets where music throws rainbows on the walls, and stands in the plaza while every window lights and a crowd raises its arms. It is the suite's one utopia: populated on purpose, and ending at the door of a house party.

Night water #05101c and deep teal #0e2a3a from ba11d121 (spired city on black water) and 18a6390f. Yellow brick #e2b33c for the causeway. Copper window-light #d98c4a from ba11d121's lit towers. Neon pink #e05aa8 is the plaza's rose stone (7fae27c3) and the signage; teal #2fb7b0 the light fence of 7f7e3980 and the crystal hall fd7ea369. Wet marble #efe9e0 for domes and the gate. Candle #ffb547 for the lantern-windows and smiles at night. The film's district colour #bb7362 is the dusty rose that ties pink stone to copper light. One accent only: the comet spark that becomes the dome beacon.

06 crosses three districts and each needs its own light rule on the cards. The Light Gate & Central Plaza: white glare and wet marble, the gate a ceramic ring with one cyan seam top to bottom; paint the plaza floor white and the terraces rose (#866f6a into #e05aa8), light the gate from inside (a back-lit vellum ring), and keep the Perimeter fence cyan and high-frequency (flicker coded at 2 frames). Cortex Commons: a blue square with warm windows and a forum lit from below; the dishes are rings, so let a radio dish card turn over the 360 orbit. Naraka Market: stacked teal and pink shipping containers, corrugated awnings, cable webs and string lights, candles in alleys two people wide; rust plated in white ceramic with a cyan Glazier seam at each container door, and the salt line chalked (never painted) at 4 m. Never show daylight; the market keeps itself at twilight by its own light. Water is everywhere underfoot: a glossy floor card under every street so neon doubles.

Insights

  • One number moves the whole poem: the waterline. Build the city once and slide the water; never re-draw buildings per movement.
  • The yellow-brick road is a causeway out over the water, so M1 is already about the sea; keep the water in frame from the first shot.
  • Atlantis must be crowded: a utopia with one figure reads as loneliness. Cheap crowd strips beat a few detailed extras.
  • The 06 reading opens with 'The gates open with wings...' (00:00-00:12): the 05 gate opening should sit on this line, then the causeway; the 'here we are all one' stanza is spoken twice in the recording, so M2 and M3 can repeat the same shot with the water lower.
  • Rainbows and long shadows are light, not objects: do them as gobo cards over the street, not as painted arcs.
  • Keep the accent budget to one thing (the comet spark -> dome beacon); neon colour is ambient, not accent.
  • Atlas: 06 travels through three districts (Light Gate/Plaza, Cortex Commons, Naraka). Change the floor and the light rule at each: white marble and glare, blue square with underlit forum, wet asphalt and neon. The floor card tells the audience where they are.
  • Atlas: sets/194b58cb993a3a33 is already a torn-paper cut-out of the rising city (rose towers, teal water, plank causeway): use it as the texture target for the whole waterfront.
  • Atlas: archive/1112 (backs climbing a warm porch under string bulbs) is the exact end frame of 06; build the House door so silhouettes pass in front of a warm back plate.

For later poems

  • Atlantis streets, plaza and the aerial are where 07 (house party), 08 (rooftop club, aerial, night street), 09 (walking with the EX under the moon) and 10 (riding out at dusk) happen.
  • The party doorway is 07's entrance.
  • The skyline row and the waterline card give 08 its top-down aerial and 14 its final harbour view.
  • The comet/beacon spark echoes the soul light of 08.
