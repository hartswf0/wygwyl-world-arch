Movement SOUTHERN PINE (15s)

Scene 05.2

"The past, too, was waiting for me— / coming of age in the shared rooms of humid basements as bedrooms; / mattress pads where the wood beams of Southern pine could comfort my spine— / it didn't. / To dream was to prevail."

Staging Treatment: No staging note beside these lines (p.10-11); staged from the line, within the night of the parade: the past waiting for him — the humid basement bedroom of his coming of age, the constellation now sawn into Southern-pine joists overhead.
