POEM 04 · Mirror Thread Bay

Nevermore

One trail followed twice, each time ending in a body part taken back and sworn over. On a night shore of ash and tambourine pieces, a man in red and black with his hands in his pockets and a hollow chest finds his own heart throbbing in the sand; it floats up and re-enters him, and he says nevermore. He follows it up an incline into a moonlit garden where faith scatters as mustard seeds and golden blooms turn to face him, and in the deepest dark he finds his two hands palm-down on bare ground, puts them back on, stands, and says it again.

Night navy and moon silver come from 3da7e92e (the moon, used in the film cut) and ee11f371 (the night garden). Ash greys (#8c9aa6) from the shore. #c23a2c is the costume's red piping and the red bare ground of 7a904269, where the hands are found. #d9a640 is the golden hue of the blooms and the mustard seeds. #5aa7ff is the suite's one accent and in this film it belongs only to the heart's pulse: never the moon, never the blooms. #e8e4d8 is the paper of the vow word cut out of the dark.

Two districts, one climb. Cenotaph Rail (04.0-04.3) is memorial and passage: the shore below the broken viaduct at twilight deepening to dark (19:24-21:36), wet stone, mist on the sea. It has no light technology of its own: only the dying sky, a few Warden lanterns on the shore steps and the moon. Paint it in wet slate and rust (#2a2222, #4d606b, #38434c, #818e92, accent rust #a94022); every viaduct arch carries the salt line at +4 m with carved names below it, and the broken spans are plated in white ceramic but the gaps are left open. In cardboard: a long horizontal shore strip for the lateral track, the viaduct's broken arches as a silhouette band high in frame, lanterns as tiny warm dots on the steps. The Night Field & the Ascent (04.4-04.6) is the only place in the city without lamps: stars, moon, and the heart's glow are all the light there is. Red soil under the moon, blue blossoms at the edge, rows in deep perspective, a lone still tree; palette #332123, #6f4e48, #194c97, #5f85a8, #072353, accent #2560e1. Static wides with him small; no camera move except the crane up the incline. Never show his hands before 04.5.

Insights

  • Hands in pockets is a casting gift: the arm cards end at the pocket, so the first four movements need no hand animation at all, and the reveal of empty sleeves costs one replacement.
  • Build the poem as two identical arcs in inverted material: the first half is ink on paper, the second paper cut out of ink. Reuse the trail, the kneel and the vow, only invert them.
  • Give the suite accent to the heart and nothing else; a second blue mark (moon, blooms) would steal it.
  • Never let the heart overshoot: it travels slowly and ends exactly at the chest, or the move reads as pointing.
  • The darkness only goes one way: dusk shore, moonlit field, deepest dark. Do not relight the field at the end; let the reach pool widen instead.
  • The hands must be a palm slab with short fingers, a thumb and a wrist stub; radiating fingers read as a starfish.
  • Atlas: the field is the only place in Atlantis without lamps. Light 04.4-04.6 with the moon, the stars and the heart's blue point only; any practical in frame breaks the district.
  • Atlas: foundry/04-02 and 04-04 are halfworld layouts of the two sets (ash shore with rim ring and stumps; perspective rows with floating puzzle pieces). Cut the floor cards from them.
  • Atlas: the trail up the incline can be a Glazier's cyan seam on the dune (foundry/04-01): the first cyan the film shows as a guide rather than an alarm.

For later poems

  • The red/black outfit with sunglasses starts here and is POET's costume through 14.
  • The night garden field is where 05's ancestors' parade arrives; POET walks out of it.
  • The reclaimed heart with its blue pulse can sit in the chest in every later poem as continuity.
  • The detachable hands answer 05's 'their blood flows through my hands'.
  • The raven cards are reusable for any later night exterior.
