Acting Arena, drawn from 3D

Every challenge, with the man drawn from the Bolas bank's 3D joints and painted as the cardboard puppet on the main sheet. Hands use the Crash Lab's own geometry, boots roll on heel, ball and toe, and parts are layered by depth. The head comes from the drawn banks, chosen by where it points and what the face is doing.

Timing follows This Side Up: keys on the authored beats, breakdowns where the move arcs, holds on twos, and ones only for the fastest moments. Every drawing is a real pose, never a blend. If the authored camera hid a face on the beats, the camera was turned to show it.
