STAGE 04 · KINEMATIC PARENT→CHILD ORDER vs. VISUAL CUFF-TUCK DRAW Z-ORDER

Why Order of Connection & Cuff-Tuck Solve Ragged Wrists & Ankles

Live 2.5× Wrist & Ankle Seam Loupes (Solved Cuff-Tuck vs. Broken Grommet Tabs)

Why Kinematic Order ≠ Z-Order: Kinematically, Forearm drives Hand and Shin drives Boot. But if you draw the child on top of the parent, the hand's brown mounting tab and the boot's rectangular pull-tab cover the ribbed sleeve/pant cuffs! Reversing the local extremity Z-order (Forearm Cuff OVER Hand, Shin Cuff OVER Boot) while clipping the parent's bottom grommet tab creates a seamless 2.5D telescopic socket across every rotation angle.
