Production design for where you go when you leave: every poem read for what it needs, and matched against what we already have: 184 generated clips, 100 Atlantis views, 15,149 found-film clips and the painted character sheets. Each poem has a page: its reading, look, best elements, characters, props, places, effects, the puppet passage, scene ideas, references to find and a kit checklist. Each character has a kit page across the poems.

Poems

01 Out of Life

A man reading his poem to a lover who keeps his back turned watches him go off the fire escape, follows him down through the world's escapes into a party that will not hold him, and falls out of life.

10 to make4 have9 props

02 Flashing Lights

Sleepless after midnight, a man takes a silent emergency call at his own mirror and falls through his dilated eye back to the winter night he was six, when a ghost child told him his parents' marriage would not last and the car's flashing lights proved it.

10 to make3 have8 props

03 How to break off an engagement

An engagement is broken off as a temple decays: the tambourine that was the ring of the promise sounds through an empty church, a storm turns love into ruined goods, and two sets of footprints leave while the tambourine shatters.

10 to make4 have13 props

04 Nevermore

Following the broken pieces to a night shore, the poet finds his own heart and then his own two hands lying where he left them, puts each back on with care, and says 'nevermore' until he can say 'Now go.'

13 to make3 have12 props

05 Bloodlines

Bloodlines starts with a man asking permission to inherit stars and ends with him warning everyone: the ancestors' gifts turn out to be the blood already in his hands.

9 to make3 have8 props

06 Resurrecting Atlantis

Resurrecting Atlantis is the arrival: winged marble gates open, a drowned city rises from black water, and a candlelit city of narrow streets takes him in, while the last line admits it was built out of 'the purest of fictions'.

6 to make3 have8 props

07 DJ Turn Me Up

At a house party a man steps to the bar mic, asks the DJ to turn him up, tells eleven years of love in pictures on the wall, and ends with his chin raised and the volume up.

7 to make2 have11 props

08 Newly Single

Newly single, he sends his soul out of his body on a rooftop dance floor, out to a burning planet to be cooled by angels' moon-salted ice water, and brings it back to breathe in the night.

6 to make2 have6 props

09 Yet, Heard

A man accepts that his love will never be returned and, before leaving, calls on the moon, his mother and his father, then walks east into a morning where he is broken and hurt, but heard.

10 to make2 have9 props

10 Magic Ride

Alone after the breakup, a man finds a motorcycle he wasn't looking for and rides it out of the night city, through loneliness and swagger, into a morning he can believe in.

7 to make2 have7 props

11 New Day

The morning after the storm, he walks out of the fog onto the wrecked chapel shore, says 'We can rebuild this temple', and the white temple rises with his hands until he can welcome the new day with his arms open.

12 to make3 have8 props

12 Reunion

Decades later, the friends fill the white temple POET rebuilt. Between the laughing, the hugs and the football, he looks into the empty rooms of the dead and holds his glass of burning sand while it runs.

9 to make2 have8 props

13 How to Win My Heart

A man alone at a corner café at the Harbor of the Heavens lists his wonders and his losses, then, on a single 'Yet', lets a distant window refocus his eyes into a smile.

11 to make6 have15 props

14 Hot Minute

As the haze takes the harbor and then him, the poet refuses to leave, curses the ending, blesses the one he loved ('I trust that you'll be fine'), and chooses the door into a slow dance as the suite loops.
