THE WOMAN, the puppet the arena used to call 'the poet' (renamed: the poet is now the man). A Black woman, dark brown skin, a full rounded afro, eyes OPEN. In the poems she plays the women: the MOTHER (09), SHE in the far window (13).

09 Yet, Heard

partial

MOTHER: weeps on the receiver, prays in the armchair, dissolves when he hangs up

Costume. mother_cardigan: long grey cardigan over a house dress, slippers; full rounded afro

Expressions. sad, weeping (folded forward), eyes closed in prayer

Hands. grip(corded_receiver), cup (prayer), relaxed

Actions. folds forward weeping; dissolves dot by dot

Cards. grey cardigan body (threequarter stand, lean, sit); head: weeping eyes closed; head: sad eyes open; hands: cupped prayer pair; hand: grip receiver
