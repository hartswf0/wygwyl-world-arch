01 Out of Life

partial

03 How to break off an engagement

partial

The one breaking off the engagement. He appears only at the end, walking away on his own trail, and in the passage, standing in the wreck with an open hand.

Costume. black_tshirt: plain black crew-neck t-shirt, dark trousers and plain dark sneakers, no glasses. Paint the shirt with a dull mud-grey splash at the hem and sneaker toes after the storm, and leave a dark wet patch across the shoulders.

Expressions. sad (eyes lowered, mouth closed), head down (replacement), profile looking back at the pool

Hands. relaxed (at sides, walking), open, palm up at waist (holds nothing)

04 Nevermore

partial

The speaker, alone: walks the shore heartless with his hands hidden, reclaims his heart, follows it into the field, finds his missing hands and puts them back on, then is told to go.

Costume. red_black_sunglasses: black track jacket with red piping running from the shoulders down the chest, black track trousers, black Chelsea boots, black sunglasses as a separate plug-in card. Dust the cuffs and knees with ash grey from the shore and soil brown after 04.5.

Expressions. sad (mouth down, head bowed; eyes hidden by glasses), listening (head tilted, mouth slightly open), recognition (chin lifts), awe (head up, mouth open), strain (jaw set, head low), afraid (mouth open, head back), calm (neutral mouth, chin level)

Hands. in_pocket (sleeve ends tucked into pocket card), missing (sleeve cuff ending in a bare kraft cut, brad hole empty), open (palm out, after re-seating), relaxed (at sides, final stand)

Actions. walk, hands in pockets; heart returns; the empty sleeves; put the hands back on

Cards. walk·threequarter and walk·profile with hands in pockets (4 leg positions); torso front with chest hollow cut-out + heart-seated torso; forearms: pocket-tab, missing-cuff, normal wrist; sunglasses card (front, threequarter, profile); heads: sad, listening, recognition, awe, strain, afraid, calm, down; kneel·threequarter, kneel·profile, crouch·front, crouch·profile, rise in-betweens; hand_L/hand_R as dusty props + open/relaxed seated versions

05 Bloodlines

partial

The heir. He walks out of the field into the parade, is handed stars, remembers the basement he grew up in, takes the ancestors' blood into his hands and turns to warn.

Costume. Black track jacket with red piping from the shoulders down the chest, black track trousers, black Chelsea boots, and black sunglasses as their own plug-in card. In the field, paint a lantern-gold rim on the parade-side edges. In the basement, use the same cards with no rim and a blue-grey wash.

Expressions. awe (chin up, mouth slightly open), joy (head back, smile) when naming a star, tired (basement, mouth slack), sad (profile, lips pressed) for 'it didn't', down (head lowered to the palms) for the passage, stern (chin down, mouth set) for 'Beware of me'

Hands. cup(star_orb) both hands, open(star_orb) one hand raised, open(universe_sphere), open palms-up, held (passage), point: a fingertip touching a node (blessing), fist (lying restless), flat_push (pushing off the pad)

Actions. receive the star; name a star; restless on the pad; hands open, held (passage); beware

Cards. lie-on-back top-down body; lie-on-side profile body; head down; head back (looking up, joy); head stern front; profile head mouth open (baritone); palms-up hand pair; cup hand pair with a slot for the star wire; extreme-close palm card with fingerprint whorls and a lifeline channel

06 Resurrecting Atlantis

partial

The arriving traveller. He walks the comet road, watches the city rise, pays the coin, walks the candlelit streets, unclenches in the plaza and stops at a door.

Costume. Black track jacket with red piping from the shoulders down the chest, black track trousers, black Chelsea boots, sunglasses as their own plug-in card. Add a second sunglasses card with two small pink/teal neon highlights for the city shots, and a gold rim on the city-facing edge.

Expressions. awe (head up to the comets), joy (open smile, arms open at the waterline), calm (closed mouth, chin level), braced (chin down, jaw set), head up (released, 'stay here forever')

Hands. relaxed, open (arms open), pinch(relearned_coin), fist at chest (braced), open at chest (released), in_pocket, reach-stopping-short (fingers slightly curled)

Actions. walk the comet road; arms open at the waterline; pay the coin; braced to open; reach that stops short (passage)

Cards. walk cycle legs (threequarter back); head back-of-head looking up; head braced; head lifted; sunglasses card with neon reflections; pinch(coin) hand; fist-at-chest and open-on-chest hands; reach-stopping-short hand; sitting replacement (on a step); arms-up pose arms

11 New Day

partial

Costume. red_black_sunglasses: black track jacket, high collar, red piping running from the shoulders down the chest; black track trousers; black Chelsea boots; black sunglasses as a separate plug-in card. 12 Reunion

partial

Host of the reunion in the temple he rebuilt: he greets, hugs, laughs, cheers, drifts into the empty rooms, closes his grip on a burning hourglass, and lays his ear on his brother's chest

Costume. red_black_sunglasses: black track jacket with red piping from the shoulders down the chest, black track trousers, black Chelsea boots; sunglasses as a plug-in card. Here the sunglasses come off (pushed up or in hand) for the hug and the ear-to-soul, so his eyes carry the 'heart overheard' beat

Expressions. joy laughing (head back, mouth open), recognition (soft smile, brows up), listening (eyes half-closed, head tilted), tired / drifting (eyes unfocused, mouth slack), sad (head lowered), calm (closed smile)

Hands. open (greeting, hug, arms up), peace (photo pose), cup(hourglass) → grip closed around it, relaxed, arm over brother's shoulder

Actions. Grip closes on the burning hourglass (passage, L03); Hug with no reason; Ear upon the next brother's soul; Laugh out loud; Restage the old photo; Arms out to the widening distance

Cards. head joy laughing (head back); head listening (tilted, eyes closed, no sunglasses); head tired/drifting; head lowered; sunglasses pushed-up card; hand cup(hourglass) L; hand grip-closed over hourglass L; peace hand R; over-shoulder arm (front); jacket hem flap

14 Hot Minute

partial

the man who built this world, is taken by its haze, floats, watches his life as a reel, and chooses the door

Costume. red_black_sunglasses suit (black track jacket with red piping, black track trousers, Chelsea boots), but sunglasses OFF throughout (taken off in 13.5c; his eyes must watch the reel). In the haze, the piping is the last colour to grey out

Expressions. reluctant (jaw set, 14.2b), softened (jaw released, eyes lowered), calm (float), recognition (eyes open, watching the reel), joy (dance), awe (at the door), gray-touched beard variant (14_L14–15)

Hands. relaxed, open (half-open, swaying, 14.2a), open palm-up (passage), hidden (inside haze), grip (hand in hand with the double), grip(vintage_door knob)

Actions. taken from the ground up; float; hand opens (passage); entwined dance; choosing the door

Cards. float bodies: profile, threequarter, front; front stand arms half-open; heads: reluctant, softened, recognition eyes-open no glasses, joy, awe, gray-touched beard; hands: palm-up open, grip knob, grip hand-in-hand, arms-up pair; back walk body
