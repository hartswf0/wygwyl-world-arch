Angular Drawing First — Not Image Shaking: In accordance with ANGULAR_STUDIO_PROMPT.md, animation in this universe is made of actual drawn cartoon cells through time. The primitives are lit surfaces, clipped angular edges (6–30 vertices per mass), and faceted light planes. Resting regions keep their shape; moving bodies receive drawn key poses on twos (contact, compression, pass, flight).
Optical Flow & Shape-Locking: Keyframes are cartoonified through SAM, OneFormer, and DepthPro decompositions, and propagated with dense optical flow across the in-betweens. The resulting animation preserves the hand-drawn cel character while following the physical motion of the filmed archive.
