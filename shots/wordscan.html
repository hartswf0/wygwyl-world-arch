Word Scan

The shot lab has already named what is in each picture and measured how far away it is. Here the picture is rebuilt from only that: every cell is a letter of the thing it belongs to, placed at its measured distance. A pulse travels out from the camera; what it has reached is wet and legible, what it has not is noise.

How to read it

NOUNS

Each cell is a letter of what the detector or the scene segmenter called it. Small named things (a neon sign, a street lamp) sit on top of the large ones (floor, wall, sky).

THE POEM

Where the scene book ties this shot to a scene of a poem, the picture is written in that scene's lines instead.

LIDAR

The same cells pushed out to their measured depth with the shot's own focal length, on every second row, so the ground reads as scan rings around the camera. The view swings to show it is a place.

The pulse

Water leaves the camera and reaches near things first. Drag WATER, or leave AUTO on.
