POEM 11 — New Day
48 image sheets

POET CONTINUITY
- The same Black male Poet throughout: deep dark-brown skin, low faded haircut, neat full beard and moustache.
- Plain black T-shirt, dark trousers, plain dark sneakers; no sunglasses.
- Black tracksuit with thin red piping, black Chelsea boots; sunglasses are a separate component.
- Young Poet is the same character as a Black boy.
- The double and soul keep the Poet identity; they are instances or special treatments of him.
- Women and other men are separately labeled supporting roles, never substitutes for the Poet.

HOW TO USE
1. Open Sheet_Index.txt to find a labeled component or pose sheet.
2. Use detached working cards, not the assembled overview figure.
3. Connect head to neck; upper arms at shoulders; forearms at elbows; hands at wrists.
4. Connect torso waist to the upper hip-bridge pivot, then each thigh to a lower hip pivot.
5. Connect shins at knees and feet at ankles. Add the matching clothed seat and separate garment panels.
6. Select the appropriate body view and pose replacements. Leave held objects separate from hands.
7. Extract, size and align parts before animation. Assembly_Guide.svg gives the joint relationships.

WHAT IS DELIVERED
- Labeled raster concept/production-reference sheets with visually separated components, not individual transparent PNG exports.
- Artwork was visually checked for identity, component classes and separation. Print scale, crop boundaries and exact pivot coordinates are not calibrated.
- Main-pose and overview figures demonstrate assembly; use the detached working parts for construction.
- Cut or mask parts, align the same-view pivots, set layer order and test motion before production.
- Effects described as coded in the treatment are represented by visual elements/instructions; no animation engine or coded effect is implemented in these packs.
