POET — BLACK / RED TRACKSUIT 31 INDIVIDUAL TRANSPARENT COMPONENTS The established adult Black male Poet: dark-brown skin, short faded haircut, neat black beard, black tracksuit with red piping and black Chelsea boots. Sunglasses remain removable. START HERE Unzip the complete folder and open index.html for the labeled visual inventory. Every working component is an individual PNG in 01_COMPONENTS. The original master and Poem 04 main-pose sheets are in 02_POSE_REFERENCES. The source sheets used for generation are in 03_SOURCE_SHEETS. These pose/source sheets have opaque backgrounds; the 31 working PNGs have alpha. L/R CONVENTION L and R always mean the Poet's anatomical left and right. In a front view, his left arm and leg appear on the viewer's right, and his right limbs on the viewer's left. This export corrects inconsistent hand labels in the old sheets. With fingers pointing down and the back of the hand facing the viewer, the L hand's thumb is on viewer-left, and R thumb on viewer-right. The open-palm hands have fingers up: L thumb viewer-left, R thumb viewer-right. TORSO AND ARMS Use 01_torso_front for the front view. 02_torso_back is a rear-view alternative. Attach each upper arm at its matching shoulder pivot, the forearm at elbow, and one chosen hand at wrist. Every upper arm and forearm is a single segment. Removing an arm at the shoulder leaves the torso usable without repainting it. Hide neck tabs beneath collars, wrist tabs beneath cuffs, and boot attachment areas beneath trouser cuffs. TWO WAIST CONNECTIONS Direct: align the torso's bottom waist pivot with the hip bridge's top-center pivot. Linked: connect the torso waist to one end of 03_waist_link, and its other end to the hip bridge's top-center pivot. This adds a waist joint and changes spacing. Choose one method for the pose. Keep the link behind the visible clothing. HIPS, LEGS AND CLOTHED COVERS 04_hip_bridge has one upper waist pivot and two lower thigh pivots. Attach a thigh to each lower pivot, then a shin at knee and a boot at ankle. For the front view, put 06_pelvis_front_cover over the bridge and upper thigh tabs. Its single waistband pivot attaches at the bridge's upper pivot, including when the waist link is used. 05_rear_seat_cover is the BACK-VIEW clothed butt/seat cover. Its rear pockets belong on the back of the character. Use it with the back torso when developing rear-view construction. It remains separate from the bridge and every leg segment. HEAD AND EXPRESSIONS Place one eye patch, one brow patch, the nose and one mouth patch on the blank head. Eye patches contain BOTH eyes; brow patches contain BOTH brows. The mouth patch supplies the moustache and central mouth-area beard. Sunglasses are the top removable face accessory. Alternative hands, expressions and front/back covers replace one another. ASSEMBLY STATUS These are separate generated PNG artworks. Set a common character scale, align the joint centers, choose layer order and check overlaps before animation. Canvas dimensions do not establish assembled scale. Pivot coordinates and motion have not been calibrated or tested. The original master and Poem 04 main-pose sheets are identity, costume and pose references; they are not newly assembled proofs made from these 31 PNGs. The two front boots and blank head use solid brass pivot markers rather than open eyelet holes. Use the marker centers when placing animation joints, or mark/punch those centers separately for physical construction. GENERATION AND REUSE Created with the built-in image-generation tool from the established Poet sheets. The exact per-component prompts are in prompts.json. Source file mapping and PNG dimensions, alpha checks, hashes and visual review notes are in manifest.json. The black/red tracksuit identity is shared across the Poem 04–14 source packs. These general front parts do not replace poem-specific actions, props or effects.
