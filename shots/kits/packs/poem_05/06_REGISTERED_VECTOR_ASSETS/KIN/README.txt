POEM05 — BLOODLINES — REGISTERED KIN MOTIF

The source specifies one nine-node, twelve-tie graph repeated in four materials.
KIN_geometry.json contains the exact normalized coordinates, named endpoints and claim order.

Each material folder contains one complete transparent SVG overlay, nine individual node SVGs and twelve individual tie SVGs. All share a 1000 x 650 viewBox so components align without guessing. Keep their full canvases when importing. Do not auto-trim individual parts. Apply the same overall scale and position to every component of an instance.

Nameplates are independent SVG overlays. Their typography and label offsets are presentation choices; the graph nodes and connectivity are exact. The colored materials are construction overlays, not raster texture replacements. Mask them into the appropriate scenery or hand image.

Show a tie only after both endpoints are named. Naming order: MAE, OTIS, RUTH, IDA, AMOS, JUNE, SOL, ELI, CORA. Only MAE receives #ffc94a. In movement3 omit that inherited-gold accent.

These vector components supplement the image-generation sheets. They do not calibrate the body-sheet pivots or make a complete animation rig.
